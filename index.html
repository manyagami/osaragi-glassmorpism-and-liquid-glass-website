About Osaragi

Osaragi is one of the newest members of The Order, the top assassin unit under the Japanese Association of Assassins (JAA) in Sakamoto Days.

She wears a black gothic-lolita dress and veil hat, speaks in a flat, quiet tone, and regularly gets distracted by snacks, hot meals, or odd superstitions between jobs. Once a fight starts, though, she doesn't hesitate—swinging a heavy motorized circular saw with ease and wiping out targets before they can react.
